# Brand

Delivery Exception Desk should feel like a calm operations desk for bilingual
e-commerce support teams.

## Typography and layout

- Keep ticket text, case IDs, and tracking IDs easy to scan.
- Use right-to-left layout for Arabic. Keep order IDs, tracking IDs, and
  policy references legible alongside Arabic text.

## Colors and surfaces

- Use white case panels, slate text, and deep teal primary actions.
- Use amber for cases awaiting evidence or review. Pair status colors with
  text labels.
- Keep the queue easy to scan and show the evidence alongside the proposed
  action in the case view.

## Tone

- Use short, specific labels in Arabic and English. Describe what the handler
  needs to do next.
- When drafting customer-facing language, match the customer's language and
  state the next step plainly.
- Display amounts in SAR.
